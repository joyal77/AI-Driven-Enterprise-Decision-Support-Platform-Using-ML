Introduction: Problem & Motivation
Problem Domain Overview
• Subscription Economy: Modern B2B SaaS, Telecom, and 
Finance rely on recurring customer revenue for long-term 
survival.
• High Churn Cost: Acquiring a new customer costs 5x to 
7x more than retaining an existing account. A 5% churn 
reduction boosts profit by 25%–95%.
• Decision Support Needs: Executive leadership requires 
automated platforms combining predictive scoring with 
actionable retention strategy.
• Proactive Monitoring: Legacy tools act after customer 
loss, whereas enterprise systems demand continuous risk 
identification.
Motivation Behind Selection
• Explainable AI (XAI): Traditional ML models predict 
churn probability but fail to explain why specific accounts 
are leaving.
• Bridging Data Silos: Customer databases, ML predictions, 
root cause analysis, and retention playbooks exist in 
fragmented silos.
• Prescriptive Analytics: Legacy dashboards alert 
managers of churn but offer no automated, personalized 
retention action playbooks.
• Unified Decision Platform: Building an end-to-end 
decision system: MySQL → XGBoost → Risk Tiers → Root 
Cause → SHAP → Recommendations.

Literature Review: Selected Research Papers 
1. Big Data & Explainable AI for Customer Retention (IEEE Access 2024)
• Paper Title & Ref:  "A Big Data-Driven Hybrid Model for Enhancing Streaming Service Customer Retention Through Churn Prediction 
Integrated With Explainable AI"
• Authors & Citation:  Usman Gani Joy, Kazi Ekramul Hoque, Mohammed Nazim Uddin, Linkon Chowdhury, Seung-Bo Park (IEEE Access, 
2024)
• Methodology:  Big Data-driven hybrid ML architecture combined with SHAP feature attribution.
• Key Advantage:  Connects churn prediction directly with SHAP feature importance for actionable retention decisions.
• Key Limitation:  Restricted to streaming domain; lacks automated prescriptive playbooks and live database integration.
2. XGBoost Approach for Customer Retention (IEEE InCACCT 2025)
• Paper Title & Ref:  "Predicting Bank Customer Churn: An XGBoost Approach to Enhancing Customer Retention"
• Authors & Citation:  Ruchika Bhuria, Srinivas Aluvala (IEEE InCACCT, 2025)
• Methodology:  Multi-level ML framework evaluating KNN, XGBoost, Random Forest, and SVM.
• Key Advantage:  Provides strong empirical validation for XGBoost superiority in customer churn classification.
• Key Limitation:  Focuses strictly on prediction accuracy; lacks SHAP explainability, root cause diagnosis, and action playbooks.

3. Early Warning Detection System for Churn (IEEE ICRTCST 2024)
• Paper Title & Ref:  "A Novel Machine Learning-Based Early Warning Detection System for Business Customer Churn"
• Authors & Citation:  Radha Raman Chandan, Ramaraj S., Sarishma, P. Praba Devi, et al. (IEEE ICRTCST, 2024)

• Paper Title & Ref:  "Prediction of Bank Credit Customers Churn Based on Machine Learning and Interpretability Analysis"
• Authors & Citation:  Ying Li, Keyue Yan (Data Science in Finance and Economics, 2025)
• Methodology:  XGBoost classifier + SHAP interpretability for bank credit churn detection.
• Key Advantage:  Uses XGBoost and SHAP to determine how individual variables influence churn (~97% AUC).
• Key Limitation:  Purely diagnostic research study; lacks automated prescriptive action playbooks and enterprise web platform.

Existing System: Overview & Critical Limitations
Existing Approaches
• Traditional BI Dashboards: Reports (Tableau/PowerBI) 
show historical churn numbers after accounts have already 
cancelled.
• Manual Monthly Audits: CSMs manually review 
customer logs using basic spreadsheet rules and subjective 
judgment.
• Isolated Statistical Models: Standard logistic regression 
scripts operate in standalone environments disconnected 
from live CRM databases.
• Unstructured Retention Process: Account intervention 
relies on ad-hoc phone calls without standardized risk-tier 
playbooks.
Critical Limitations
✕  Reactive Detection: Churn is detected after accounts 
have ceased activity, missing critical early intervention 
windows.
✕  Black-Box AI Opacity: ML models output raw risk scores 
(e.g., 85% risk) without explaining individual feature 
drivers.
✕  Manual Intervention: Account managers must 
manually investigate churn causes, delaying outreach by 
days or weeks.
✕  Data Fragmentation: CRM logs, predictive models, and 
retention steps exist in silos, incurring 5x-7x re-acquisition 
costs.

=== PAGE 10 ===
Methodology & Core ML/XAI Modules
Predictive & XAI Modules
• XGBoost Classifier Model: Gradient boosted decision 
trees optimized via Optuna hyperparameter tuning for 
churn probability scoring.
• SHAP TreeExplainer: Computes local Shapley values 
breaking down individual feature impacts (+/-) per 
customer account.
• Class Imbalance Handling: Utilizes SMOTE (Synthetic 
Minority Over-sampling Technique) to balance churn and 
non-churn classes.
Diagnostic & Prescriptive Modules
• Root Cause Diagnostic Engine: Evaluates top negative 
SHAP features against operational telemetry (support 
tickets, payment delays, login drops).
• Prescriptive Action Engine: Rule-driven recommendation 
engine mapping root causes to automated playbooks (CSM 
assignment, discounts, training).
• Executive SPA Dashboard: Modern web dashboard 
serving interactive SHAP waterfall plots, risk tables, and 
retention workflow cards.
